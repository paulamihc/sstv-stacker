SSTV Stacker

Combine repeat copies of an SSTV picture into one better copy. Nothing made up.

1. Choose your pictures

Choose a folder of SSTV pictures: PNG, JPEG or BMP, from any decoder. It can hold several different pictures, each received a few times; SSTV Stacker works out which copies go together. Folders inside it are read too.

Options

Robot 36 sends red and blue colour on alternate lines. If a decoder loses track of which is which, the colours swap (orange shows as blue). Other modes can't swap like that.

3. Your best copies

    Nothing made up

    1. Every pixel comes from your copies. At each place, the copies that agree are averaged, weighted by how cleanly each line came in. Every value lies between the lowest and highest your copies hold there. No AI, no filling in, no upscaling, no guessing between lines.
    2. A line no copy received stays missing, shown as a grey hatched band.
    3. Only decoding errors are undone: copies are lined up (a shift of a few pixels), and swapped Robot 36 colours are put back.

    Each combined picture keeps the details of its strongest copy (such as time, frequency and callsign), with four added: which copies it was combined from, the strongest copy, the coverage, and what combined it. A record beside it says how each line was made. A picture received only once is passed on exactly as it was.

    Private

    Everything happens in your browser. Your pictures are never uploaded: this page has no server to send them to, no tracking and no analytics.
